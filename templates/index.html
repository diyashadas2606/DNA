How was the model trained?

400 trees. A stratified 80/20 split with seed 42. Five-fold validation within the 492 training records. Median imputation and sex encoding are fitted inside each fold. The saved model is the same one evaluated on the 123 test records.

Impurity importance describes the fitted trees, not causal or patient-specific medical importance.
